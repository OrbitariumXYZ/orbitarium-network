Short answers.

Where does the lower price come from?
Holders and allowance partners hand over capacity they are not using, below what they paid for it. Our margin is folded into the quote you see — there is no separate charge.
Is the model the genuine one?
Yes. Orbitarium forwards each call to the route you addressed and hands the reply straight back, upstream label and identifier intact. There is no quiet substitution to a lower tier.
Do you keep what I send?
No. The relay records billing facts only: the route, the token tallies, the charge and the clock. Wording in and wording out pass through and are not stored anywhere.
Whose allowance covers my calls?
Either Orbitarium's own capacity or a vetted partner's paid allowance. Partner traffic runs through that partner's upstream account under its own retention terms. What is reachable depends on the route.
What has to change in my code?
Two values: the call's base location and your Orbitarium credential. Streaming, function calls, image input and route identifiers all behave as they do now.
Any recurring fee or floor?
Neither. Put in a modest figure and draw against it call by call. Anything left over simply waits there for next time.
What does the relay add per call?
Your account view reports the measured relay overhead for your own traffic, call by call.
Can a balance be cashed out?
No. Balance is a prepaid grant of access, not a stake and not convertible back to money. If a card charge misfires, reply to the receipt and the processor reverses it.

Balance is released by holders and allowance partners, settled on Robinhood Chain.
